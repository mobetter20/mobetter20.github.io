10 Reasons Why You Shouldn’t Read This List

Reason number zero, click bait is ruining this country

Originally published on Medium on September 30, 2022. View original

The scourge of listicles is ruining the internet.

So to save the masses from themselves, I wrote down the following points, arranged both sequentially and vertically.

10 Reasons Why You Shouldn’t Read This List

  1. Your literacy is not on a per-word limited plan.
  2. Your literacy IS on a per-word limited plan.
  3. You have enough procrastination tabs open already.
  4. You still have free articles left in The New Yorker.
  5. You could be shaking the crumbs out of your keyboard instead.
  6. You are asking why you are reading this.
  7. You will forget that you’ve read this in about an hour (starting — now)
  8. This list stopped being funny at number one. Possibly at zero.
  9. You are still here just out of spite.
  10. You have now finished the list without wasting any time. Much.
