Farewell to The Fork

The perils of eating at the desk

Originally published on Medium on March 15, 2023. View original

Farewell to the fork that I let slip,
Down behind the cabinet, oh what a trip!
I was eating at my desk, in a rush to complete,
And without a second thought, I let you retreat.

Now you lie in the darkness, all on your own,
Forgotten and neglected, without any tone.
I know I could save you, dig you out with ease,
But the thought of getting down there, oh, it makes me freeze.

I could move the cabinet, search high and low,
But let’s be honest, that’s not where I wanna go.
I’ll just use a spoon or knife, they’ll do just fine,
And pretend I never lost you, everything’s just fine.

So farewell to the fork that fell from my grasp,
May you find a new home, without any past.
I’ll think of you fondly, when I eat my next meal,
But for now, I’ll just use what I have, it’s no big deal.
