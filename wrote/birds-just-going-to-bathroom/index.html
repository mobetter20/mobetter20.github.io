Birds Just Going to Bathroom

Birds just like going together

Originally published on Medium on November 14, 2022. View original

Survey shows that not all birds flying in majestic formations are on an epic journey across oceans.

“Most of the time, it’s just a bathroom trip,” says Marline A l’Orange.

“You get sick of shitting where you eat pretty quickly. And we like going to the bathroom together. It’s a bonding thing.”

“Not everything in life has to be an epic journey,” added Debora Confit, the number two in their formation. “Big or small, it’s all call of nature”.
