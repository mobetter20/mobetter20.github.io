The Queen is Dead, Long Live The Queue

Feeling lost? Join the queue

Originally published on Medium on September 19, 2022. View original

As soon as I heard the news that the Queen had died I was immediately in a queue, not ‘The Queue’ mind you, I was just at Heathrow airport.

I staggered at the sheer unexpectedness of the news and tripped over the pile of suitcases to land on my knees — lining myself up for a 3-month queue just to see my GP.

So when I finally joined The Queue, I was already in waiting.

With her majesty gone, I was utterly lost. HM Queen Elizabeth II was not only a daily inspiration but an example to follow in my day-to-day struggle as a 42 years old single mom juggling two jobs.

Then I realise with ‘The Queue’, I only need to follow the person in front of me.

All I had to do was to keep in line, and the clear social order telling me who comes first would make me feel right at home.

Some say queue is a symbol of ineffectual distribution of resources — millions of people waiting up to 24 hrs to see a coffin of a 96 years old dead woman — but this is what the empire was built on. Queue is in our blood, sweat and tear — it’s in our water.

Then it came to me that we don’t need Charles — thank god for that — and the true succession was ‘in line’, The Queue.

That’s why I’m still here — with my bad knees — to pay respect, and to respect The Queue. And most importantly, to behold the majesty of The Queue.
