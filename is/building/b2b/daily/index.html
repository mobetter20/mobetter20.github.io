How to play

Tap an available word to change your bird. Each tap makes one move.

Common Gull → Little Gull

Reach the goal bird in as few moves as you can.

Grey words cannot make a bird at this step. You can reuse words; going back counts as a move.

Every finish counts. A shortest route earns Perfect!
